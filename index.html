Refund & Cancellation Policy

Due to the nature of live event ticketing, all confirmed ticket sales are final once digital transfer is initiated. If an event is permanently cancelled by the organizer (and not rescheduled), customers will receive a 100% refund for the ticket value. If an event is postponed or rescheduled, original tickets remain valid for the new date, and refunds are not issued.
