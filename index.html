Refund & Cancellation Policy

Due to the nature of live event ticketing, confirmed ticket sales are generally final once payment has been completed and ticket delivery or transfer has been initiated.

If an event is permanently cancelled by the event organizer and is not rescheduled, customers may be eligible for a refund of the amount paid for the affected tickets, subject to the applicable refund terms of the event organizer, ticket provider, or payment processor.

If an event is postponed or rescheduled, tickets generally remain valid for the new event date. Refund eligibility will depend on the policy of the applicable ticket provider or event organizer.

Requests to cancel an order before ticket delivery or transfer may be considered on a case-by-case basis, depending on the status of the order and whether the tickets have already been purchased or committed on the customer's behalf.
